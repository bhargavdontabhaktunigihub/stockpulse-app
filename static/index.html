Yield Trap Rule: Beware of deceptively high dividend yields (e.g. 8%–14%). When a troubled company's stock price crashes, its historical yield mechanically shoots up. If the Payout Ratio exceeds 85% or Free Cash Flow is negative, management will soon cut or suspend the dividend, sending the stock tumbling further. Always prioritize payout safety over high nominal yield.
